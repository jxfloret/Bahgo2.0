1. What BahGo is

BahGo shows water levels and rain from street sensors in Palar Village, Taguig, a 30-minute flood forecast, a flood risk level, a rain outlook from the street sensor, a 7-day weather forecast, and flood alerts.

2. Important: BahGo is not an emergency service

  • BahGo gives information only. It does not replace official warnings from PAGASA, NDRRMC, MMDA or the Taguig City government. Always follow official advisories and evacuation orders.
  • Sensors can go offline, be blocked or give wrong readings, and the internet or notifications may be delayed. A "Safe" reading does not guarantee a street is safe.
  • Forecasts and risk levels come from statistical models (Linear and Lasso Regression) and weather services. They are estimates and can be wrong.
  • The "Recorded (simulated)" data in the flood chart is sample data for demonstration, not real sensor history.
  • Never walk or drive into flood water based only on the app. In an emergency, call 911.
