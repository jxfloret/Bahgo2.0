BahGo Data Privacy Consent Form

Version 2026-10-29

In line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), please read this before creating your account or continuing to use BahGo.

What I agree to

By checking "I agree", I confirm that:

  1. I have read and understood the BahGo Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
  2. I freely give my consent to the BahGo Team (4th-year BS Computer Science students of STI College Global City) to collect, store and use my first name, surname, mobile number, password (stored only as a hash), preferences and log-in records to:
    • create and secure my account,
    • send me one-time SMS codes,
    • show flood conditions and forecasts, and send me flood alerts.
  3. I understand that my mobile number and SMS code are sent through an SMS gateway, and that my data is stored with Google Firebase in Singapore.
  4. I understand BahGo is not an emergency service: its readings, forecasts and alerts can be wrong or late, and I will follow official advisories.

Optional

Usage statistics: if I also check this box, I allow BahGo to collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase to improve the app. Leaving it unchecked does not affect any feature.

My rights

I can withdraw my consent at any time. I can turn off usage statistics and flood alerts in Settings, and ask for access to, correction or deletion of my data by writing to hannaduliesco@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent to the required items means I will no longer be able to use my account. I may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
