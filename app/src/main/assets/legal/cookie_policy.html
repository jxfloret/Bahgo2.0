BahGo Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version 2026-10-29

Does BahGo use cookies?

No. BahGo is a mobile app. It has no web pages inside it and does not use browser cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Like most apps, it does use a few similar technologies that store small pieces of information on your phone or identify your installation. This page lists them so you know what they are and how to control them.

1. Storage on your phone (strictly necessary)

These are needed for the app to work and stay on your phone:

  • Login session: keeps you signed in. Your mobile number is kept in encrypted storage.
  • Your settings: light/dark mode, Person or Car, flood alerts on/off, and whether you've seen the Get Started screens and the Home tour.
  • Recent readings: water levels and rain from the street sensors for up to 3 days, to draw the chart and make forecasts.
  • Alert memory: the last alert per street, so you don't get the same alert twice.
  • Caches: map images and the latest weather forecast, so the app is faster and works briefly offline.

2. Identifiers used by Google Firebase

  • Notification token (strictly necessary for alerts): lets flood alerts reach your phone. Turning off Flood Alerts in Settings stops alerts.
  • Analytics app-instance ID (optional): used by Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used. It is only active if you agree to usage statistics, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security. The advertising ID is not collected.

3. Outside services the app connects to

When the app loads maps, weather or search results, the provider (OpenFreeMap, Hugging Face, Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap Nominatim) sees your internet (IP) address, like any website you visit. The app does not set cookies for them and does not let them track you inside the app.

4. How to control or remove them

  • Turn usage statistics or flood alerts on or off in Settings.
  • Remove everything stored on your phone: Android Settings > Apps > BahGo > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.
