BahGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version 2026-10-29

BahGo ("BahGo", "we", "us") is a flood-monitoring app for Palar Village, Taguig City. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The personal information controller is the BahGo Team (4th-year BS Computer Science students of STI College Global City), STI Academic Center, University Parkway, Bonifacio Global City, Taguig City, Metro Manila 1634.
Data Protection Officer / privacy contact: hannaduliesco@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first name, surname and mobile number.
  • Password: we never store your password itself. We store a scrambled version (a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash) so we can check it when you log in.
  • Preferences: whether you monitor as a Person or with a Car, light/dark mode, and whether flood alerts are on.
  • Place searches: the words you type in Search (sent to OpenStreetMap to find places).

Information collected automatically

  • Login records: your mobile number and the time of each log-in, to keep your account secure.
  • Notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging identifier so we can send flood alerts to your phone.
  • Usage statistics (only if you agree): Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app-instance ID, device model, Android version, approximate country, app version and how the app is used (for example, screens opened and session length). It is off unless you turn it on during sign-up or in Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Your location: if you allow it, your phone's location is used only on your phone to show your position on the map. We do not send it to our servers or store it.

Information that is not about you
Water level and rain readings come from street sensors, not from your phone. They are not personal information.

3. Why we use it (purpose and lawful basis)

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in (your consent; performance of our service).
  • To send you a one-time SMS code when you sign up or change your number (consent; account security).
  • To show flood conditions, forecasts and send flood alerts for the monitored streets (consent; protecting life and safety).
  • To tailor advice to walking or driving (consent).
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, only if you opt in to usage statistics (consent).

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you that have legal effects. The flood forecasts are about streets, not about people.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf or receive only what is needed:

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, and Analytics if you opt in): stores your account, login records and preferences, and delivers notifications. Database location: Singapore (asia-southeast1).
  • SMS Gateway for Android (sms-gate.app): receives your mobile number and the one-time code to send it to you by SMS. If you are logged in and a monitored street reaches Critical, it also receives your number and a short flood alert to text you (at most one per street every 30 minutes).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: receives the text you search and your internet (IP) address.
  • OSRM routing (routing.openstreetmap.de and router.project-osrm.org): when you use Iwas-Baha Route, receives the start and end points of your route (which can be your current location) and your IP address, to work out walking or driving routes. We don't store your routes.
  • OpenFreeMap and Hugging Face: deliver map images and the flood-hazard map; they see your IP address and which map area is loaded.
  • Open-Meteo: provides the 7-day weather forecast for Taguig; it sees your IP address only.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a lawful order of a Philippine authority.

5. International transfer

Some providers store or process data outside the Philippines (for example, Singapore, the European Union and the United States). We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details and preferences: while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete them within 30 days.
  • Login records: up to 12 months.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months (Firebase default), and only if you opted in.
  • On your phone: recent water-level readings are kept for up to 3 days to draw the chart; you can remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.

7. How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, your session on the phone is kept in encrypted storage, and connections use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to claim damages. You can:

  • Edit your name and change your number or password in Settings.
  • Turn usage statistics and flood alerts on or off in Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for account deletion by writing to hannaduliesco@gmail.com.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

9. Children

BahGo is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app with your parent or guardian's permission.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, ask for your consent again in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: hannaduliesco@gmail.com.
